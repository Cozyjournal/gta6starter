Hey Arnold!: find Arnold and choose his fate

Spoiler warning: this guide describes one Chapter 1 story mission, a hunt for Brian's missing broker that ends in a long car chase and a choice. Short answer: Hey Arnold! starts when Brian calls after Pipework. Meet him at the Crastenburg Hotel pool bar, pick up Cal at the liquor store, track Arnold to the radar station on Maroon Key, chase him across the Keys, then decide at the docks whether he lives or dies. Everything below is Reported from launch-week walkthroughs (checked 23 November 2026); Rockstar has published no official guide of its own.

Mission facts

  • Chapter: Chapter 1, mandatory story mission, needed for 100% completion. (Reported)
  • Mission number: the 8th main mission, according to PowerPyx. Trackers disagree on exact numbering, because several Chapter 1 missions are open at once and can be played in different orders. (Reported)
  • Given by: Brian. (Reported)
  • Unlock: finish Pipework, then wait a few minutes for Brian's call. (Reported)
  • Where: starts at the Crastenburg Hotel pool bar in the south of Key Lento; Arnold is found at the radar station on Maroon Key. (Reported)
  • You play: Jason. Brian tells you to bring a gun. (Reported)
  • Reward: no fixed reward is listed. IGN reports the payment lands in your mailbox, about $1,200 combined with the Mandy's rent and narcotics jobs. One wiki reports $1,400 in some playthroughs and the Bombara S23 Riverboat unlocking. (Reported, sources differ)
  • Next mission: Island Dreams. (Reported)

Where Hey Arnold! starts

After Pipework, wait for Brian's call, then follow the marker to the Crastenburg Hotel on the south-east side of Key Lento. Brian is sitting alone in the cabana by the pool bar, angry that his long-time broker Arnold has ghosted him after the run-in with the Kingfishers. Before you set off, eat something, food from home is free and buffs your Health, Stamina and Focus, and grab an extra weapon from your own car's trunk if you want one, because this is your last chance before you get into Brian's truck, a big Vapid Caracara 4X4 parked right next to your car. Get into the driver's seat and wait for Brian to climb in. (Reported)

Step by step

  1. Meet Brian at the Crastenburg Hotel pool bar and follow him to his truck.
  2. Drive Brian's truck to the nearby liquor store and pick up Cal.
  3. Follow the pink search area south to Maroon Key until it narrows to the radar station.
  4. Walk up to Arnold at the small white-and-red building and watch the meeting go wrong.
  5. Chase Arnold's car across the Keys, over the broken bridge and the beach, until he crashes.
  6. Chase Arnold on foot to the docks and choose what to do with him.
  7. Collect your payment from the mailbox afterwards.

Before any mission like this, a manual save is cheap insurance, GTA 6 has no mission replay (Reported).

Finding Arnold at the radar station

With Cal in the truck, a pink search area appears further south, in Maroon Key. Drive into it and the marker narrows to Arnold's exact position as you get near, a small white-and-red building by the radar station, with a tethered blimp (aerostat) above it. On the way you may get a prompt to look at the aerostat off to the west. Cal says the last ping from Arnold's phone came from this radar station. Pull up and you will see three men standing around a dirty black truck. In the cutscene Brian confronts Arnold, who is unhappy about how the Kingfishers deal went and makes it clear he is done working with Brian. Then Arnold's people start shooting. (Reported)

The chase

Arnold drives off, so follow him. Two motorcycles join the chase, each with a driver and a gunner shooting at you. You can shoot back while driving: press L1/LB to draw your weapon and R1/RB to fire, and press L3 + R3 to slow down time, which makes the shots easier. Brian leans out and joins the gunfight too. Our controls guide covers drive-by shooting in full. (Reported)

Drive the truck carefully. Brian's Caracara does not handle well at high speed, it is easy to flip it or end up in the bushes, and hitting a telephone pole or a thick tree at speed can total the truck and force a checkpoint restart. Use the brake for smoother turns. Small traffic signs and thin fences topple easily, so you can ram through those. (Reported)

The route takes you across an old bridge with gaps, your first stunt jumps. Go as straight as possible: landing at an angle can wedge the truck between the concrete barriers. If you get stuck, Arnold seems to drive slower while you are not directly behind him, so you get recovery time. After the bridge, Arnold crosses a short stretch of beach and is joined by two more motorcycles. The beach is one of your best chances to get close and shoot out the tyres on Arnold's car, if you manage it, he is forced to stop. If you don't, don't worry: you get other chances to confront him. A white van with armed men hanging out the back joins the chase through the neighbourhood and out onto the main street of Key Lento. Eventually Arnold's truck veers off the road and smashes into a small white building. He scrambles out and the chase continues on foot. (Reported)

The foot chase and the choice at the docks

Chase Arnold through the neighbourhood to the docks, sprint to keep up, and don't let him get too far away or he escapes. You can shoot him to end it fast, or keep chasing to see how it plays out. At the end of the dock Jason automatically pulls out his pistol and Arnold drops to his knees, trying to explain himself. Hold the prompt button and focus on Arnold to get the option to spare him. (Reported)

Kill or spare, what changes: either choice finishes the mission and the story continues to Island Dreams. Arnold leaves the game permanently either way, if you let him go, he swims away and disappears for good; Jason tells Brian he will never cause trouble again, and that is good enough for Brian. The difference is in your criminal profile: how you handle criminal situations shifts it, and this choice is one of the moments that counts. (Reported)

What happens after

A cutscene shows Jason back at Brian's house for a drink, where he meets Brian's friend and associate Kenton, and they talk about treasure hunting. Then you get control of Jason at home, working out, finish the workout if you can, because working out improves health and stamina, and you have a limited window to earn progress towards the next level. Visit the mailbox to collect your payment for this mission (see the reward note above). You may also get a text from Donnie about striking back at the Kingfishers by hitting their operations in the grasslands. (Reported)

Fail conditions

  • Totalling Brian's truck on a telephone pole or a thick tree at speed, restart from the last checkpoint. (Reported)
  • Letting Arnold get too far away during the foot chase. (Reported)
  • If you get completely stuck, pause and choose "Retry Checkpoint" or "Restart Mission" from the pause menu. (Reported)

Previous and next missions

Hey Arnold! unlocks after Pipework. In the order most trackers list Chapter 1, the next mission is Island Dreams, but several Chapter 1 missions are open at the same time, so your own order may differ. See the full list on our GTA 6 missions page. (Reported)

FAQ

How do you start Hey Arnold! in GTA 6?
Finish Pipework, then wait a few minutes for Brian's call. Meet him at the pool bar of the Crastenburg Hotel in the south of Key Lento. (Reported)

What is the reward for Hey Arnold! in GTA 6?
No fixed reward is listed. IGN reports the payment lands in your mailbox, about $1,200 combined with the Mandy's rent and narcotics jobs; one wiki reports $1,400 in some playthroughs and the Bombara S23 Riverboat unlocking. (Reported, sources differ)

Should you kill or spare Arnold in GTA 6?
The story continues either way and Arnold leaves the game permanently. Sparing him affects your criminal profile differently from killing him. (Reported)

How do you shoot while driving in the Hey Arnold! chase?
Press L1/LB to draw your weapon while driving and R1/RB to fire. Press L3 + R3 to slow down time and make the shots easier. (Reported)

What mission comes after Hey Arnold! in GTA 6?
Island Dreams is the next main mission. (Reported)
