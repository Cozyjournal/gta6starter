GTA 6 unlock time in Australia

GTA 6 unlocks in Australia at 12:00 AM local time on Thursday, 19 November 2026 on PlayStation — the second unlock in the world, two hours after New Zealand. Xbox unlocked at the same local midnight: 12:00 AM on 19 November in Sydney. Confirmed

Good to know in Australia
