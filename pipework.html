Pipework: Mandy's rent & the sink fix

Spoiler warning: this guide describes one short Chapter 1 story mission — a small handyman job, nothing bigger. Short answer: Pipework is a mandatory Chapter 1 story mission. On Brian's orders, Jason goes to collect rent from Mandy in Key Lento — and before she pays, he has to fix her blocked kitchen sink. Everything below is Reported from launch-week walkthroughs (checked 23 November 2026); Rockstar has published no official guide of its own.

Mission facts

  • Chapter: Chapter 1 — mandatory story mission, needed for 100% completion. (Reported)
  • Mission number: early Chapter 1. Trackers disagree (5, 6 or 7), because several missions are open at once and can be played in different orders. (Reported)
  • Given by: Brian Heder sends you; the job itself is for Mandy. (Reported)
  • Where: Mandy's house in Key Lento, Mariana County. (Reported)
  • You play: Jason.
  • Reward: no cash reward listed — one optional pickup, a Macizo cigar. (Reported)
  • Gold medal / timer: none. GTA 6 has no mission gold medals, and Pipework has no trophy of its own. (Reported)

Where Pipework starts

The mission opens in Key Lento: Brian sends you to collect the rent, and Mandy's house is marked on your map. Go there — Mandy will not hand over the rent until the kitchen sink is fixed, so the rent collection turns into a short plumbing job first. (Reported)

Step by step

  1. Go to Mandy's house in Key Lento and speak to her.
  2. Head inside and find the plunger — it is in the bathroom.
  3. Take the plunger to the kitchen and work on the blocked sink until it clears.
  4. Collect the rent from Mandy.
  5. On your way out, grab the optional Macizo cigar (below) if you want it.

Before any mission like this, a manual save is cheap insurance — GTA 6 has no mission replay (Reported).

How to fix the sink

The sink is a short interaction, not a puzzle: with the plunger in hand, use the on-screen prompt at the kitchen sink and keep going until the blockage clears. One honest warning: walkthroughs disagree about the exact control — some describe an R3/wrench prompt, others say hold R2/RT. Follow the prompt your own screen shows; the step itself is the same either way. (Reported, sources differ)

If the game itself is misbehaving rather than the mission, see our GTA 6 crashing fix guide instead.

The optional Macizo cigar

A Macizo cigar can be picked up at Mandy's house during this mission. It is a small optional pickup — pleasant to have, and completely safe to leave behind: nothing in the mission, the story or 100% completion depends on it. (Reported)

Can you miss anything?

According to the walkthroughs, no. Pipework is a mandatory story mission, so it cannot be skipped or locked out, and nothing missable hangs on it — the cigar is optional flavour, and the mission opens no romance route or side branch. Play it when you reach it and move on. (Reported)

Previous and next missions

In the order most trackers list Chapter 1, Pipework comes after Unwanted Attention and before Hey Arnold! — but several Chapter 1 missions are open at the same time (including Gear Up), so your own order may differ. See the full list on our GTA 6 missions page. (Reported)

FAQ

Is Pipework a mandatory mission in GTA 6?
Yes. Pipework is a mandatory Chapter 1 story mission and counts towards 100% completion. (Reported)

What is the reward for Pipework in GTA 6?
There is no cash reward listed; the only pickup is the optional Macizo cigar at Mandy's house. (Reported)

Where is the plunger in Pipework?
Inside Mandy's house, in the bathroom. Pick it up before working on the kitchen sink. (Reported)

Does Pipework have a gold medal or a trophy?
No. GTA 6 has no mission gold medals, and Pipework has no trophy or achievement of its own. (Reported)

Which mission number is Pipework?
Sources disagree: trackers list it as mission 5, 6 or 7. It is an early Chapter 1 mission; the exact number depends on the order you play. (Reported)
