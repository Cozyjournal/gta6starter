About GTA 6 Starter

GTA 6 Starter is an unofficial fan-made guide for people playing Grand Theft Auto VI for the first time: release and preload help, honest prices, beginner guides and mission walkthroughs in story order.

How we mark what's true

Every important fact on this site carries one of three labels, and a source with a check date at the bottom of the page:

  • Confirmed — stated by Rockstar Games or an official store.
  • Reported — published by reliable trade press but not confirmed by Rockstar. Reports are never presented as facts.
  • Not announced — Rockstar has said nothing. We say so instead of guessing.

When a fact changes, we update the page and the check date — we do not quietly delete the old information. Mission guides are written from verified gameplay information only; if we cannot verify a step, the guide waits.

Who runs GTA 6 Starter

GTA 6 Starter is an independent, unofficial fan guide, written and maintained by fans for fans. We are not Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, Sony or Microsoft, and nothing on this site is official.

Corrections & contact

Spotted a mistake? Tell us and we will check it against our sources — if we are wrong, we fix the page and say what changed. The fastest way to reach us is a comment or message on our YouTube channel, GTA 6 Starter.

Privacy policy

This site has no accounts and no sign-ups, and we never ask you for personal information. We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which guides help readers most; Google Analytics sets cookies to do that. If we show advertising in the future (for example Google AdSense), those providers may also use cookies to personalise ads — you can control or clear cookies any time in your browser settings. We do not sell personal data, because we do not collect any. Questions about privacy: use the contact route above.
