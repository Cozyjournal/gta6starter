The protagonists

Lucia

One of the two lead characters. The story opens with Lucia's release from prison, and the campaign follows her and Jason as a couple pulled into a criminal conspiracy across Leonida — Rockstar describes their situation as: when a simple score goes wrong, they find themselves on the dark side of the sunniest place in America.

Jason

The second playable lead. Jason works around the Leonida Keys and wants an easy life — but life keeps getting harder. Switching between Jason and Lucia is central to how the story is told.

Supporting cast

Rockstar's official character pages confirm more named characters alongside Jason and Lucia. Launch coverage of the released game has also put names and roles to the people around them — those are marked Reported and come from our mission and money guides, checked against launch-week sources.

Cal

Jason's friend. He rides along on Jason's first playable mission, following Donnie's plane by boat to fish dropped packages out of the sea. Reported

Donnie

A pilot whose plane drops packages over the Leonida Keys. When the Kingfishers take him, Jason rescues him from their camp in Pelican Valley. Reported

Brian

The name behind Brian's Marina, where the seaplane flight in Mission 2 ends. Reported

Mandy

Introduced in Pipework, an early Chapter 1 mission — the same mission where many players report a progress-blocking crash. Reported

Moses

Buys stolen cars from Chapter 2 onward — one of the first steady money-makers in the game. Reported

Yasmani

Sells security-van tips for $10,000 — buy one and a robbery opportunity follows. Reported

We only list characters Rockstar has officially revealed. Names from leaks or rumours are not published here as fact.

FAQ

How many playable characters are in GTA 6?

Two: Jason and Lucia. Switching between them is central to how the story is told, and missions assign who you play — for example, Unwanted Attention is played as Jason.

Who is the main character in GTA 6?

Both of them. GTA 6 follows Jason and Lucia as a couple pulled into a criminal conspiracy across Leonida — neither is a side character.

Who are Jason's friends in GTA 6?

The early game puts Jason alongside Cal Hampton, his friend who rides along on the first missions, and Brian Heder, who sends Jason work in Chapter 1 — starting with Pipework. Reported
