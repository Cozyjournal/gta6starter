GTA 6 garages: every location, price and how to keep a car

Short answer: stealing a car does not make it yours. To keep a vehicle permanently you must register it, and to register it you need a free garage slot. There are 12 garages in GTA 6 — 8 you can buy for $160,000 in total and 4 free ones from the story — giving 16 garage spaces, plus the two personal vehicles Jason and Lucia keep parked outside, for a maximum of 18 vehicles. Registration starts in Chapter 2 with Moses in Port Gellhorn (tracker-free cars only, your first one free), and Pay 'N' Spray takes over for tracked cars from Chapter 3. (Reported by PowerPyx, KeenGamer, SlashGear and TechWiser, checked 22 November 2026. Rockstar has published no official guide, so everything here is Reported.)

How to register a car (make it yours)

Registration is the whole system — without it, a stolen car is just borrowed, and it can be lost or destroyed for good. The steps, as described by KeenGamer, SlashGear and Rare Thief:

1. Find a car you want to keep. Before you steal it, scan it with the WAiNK app — hold Up on the D-pad to open the app, then scan with R1 on PS5 (RB on Xbox). The scan shows whether the car has a tracker and what registration will cost.

2. Look for Tracker: None. Moses will only register tracker-free cars at this stage. He will not touch cars with Standard or Advanced trackers.

3. Steal the car and drive it to Moses' garage — Watkins Auto Parts in Port Gellhorn, marked with the MW icon on your map. One warning from KeenGamer: Moses will not accept a vehicle stolen too close to his garage. If he refuses it, drive out to the main road through Port Gellhorn, find another tracker-free car there, and bring that one back.

4. Drive inside, respray the car (change its colour), then select Register. The car becomes your Personal Vehicle.

Your first registration with Moses is free — as he puts it, "Just this once, I'll get your ride registered for you on the house." After that, each registration costs a fee based on the car's value, shown on the WAiNK scan as Cost to Register. That makes the first free one worth spending on the most expensive tracker-free car you can find — Rare Thief's example prices run from $1,400 for a Willard '92 Grouse to $9,000 for a Bravado Bison.

Registration is unlocked by the Chapter 2 story mission Moses (Take A Vehicle to Moses), which you reach after the mission Girl's Trip. Until then, Jason's starting car is your only Personal Vehicle and nothing you steal can be registered. The mission also gives Lucia the Slim Jim, which opens locked cars without setting off their alarms, and unlocks the Scooter Bros app — the app you later use to have a registered car delivered to you. (KeenGamer, SlashGear, Rare Thief)

Trackers: which cars you can register, and where

Not every stolen car can become yours right away. It depends on the tracker, and on how far you are in the story:

  • No tracker — Moses, from Chapter 2. Drive it to Watkins Auto Parts in Port Gellhorn. First registration free, later ones cost a fee based on the car's value.
  • Standard tracker — Pay 'N' Spray, from Chapter 3. Early in Chapter 3, Pay 'N' Spray locations start registering cars with Standard trackers. Find a Pay 'N' Spray on the map under Vehicles, lose any active police pursuit first, then drive in and register. (KeenGamer, SlashGear)
  • Advanced tracker — Randy, later in Chapter 3. Cars with advanced trackers need Randy, who you meet later in Chapter 3. Complete a few missions with him and he will call to say Pay 'N' Spray workshops can now handle advanced-tracker cars too. (SlashGear)

Both Jason and Lucia can have their own personal vehicles, and registered vehicles travel with you — when Jason and Lucia move to Vice City at the end of Chapter 2, your registered cars go with them. You can also sell personal vehicles to free up a slot, and manage them through the Scooter Bros app. (SlashGear, Rare Thief)

How to unlock garages

You start with almost no storage — one ride and nowhere to keep anything else — and it opens up in three steps (TechWiser, PowerPyx):

1. Meet Moses in Chapter 2. He is the car-trade contact, and his Port Gellhorn garage is your first storage.

2. Finish the Chapter 2 mission Daily Driver (steal the Crawler for Moses). Shortly afterwards your phone gets the Vice City Real Estate app. You can buy garages through the app, or in person at the garage sites.

3. Keep playing the story. More garages unlock automatically along the way — you never have to pay for all of them.

All 8 garages you can buy

Each bought garage holds one vehicle and counts towards the Little Boxes trophy/achievement, which unlocks when you buy all eight. Together they cost $160,000. Prices and locations, confirmed across PowerPyx, ShowGamer, GamesRecon and GTA 6 Notebook:

  • Mitchell Auto Center — $4,000. Central Kelly County, east of Port Gellhorn. The cheapest garage, and the best first buy.
  • Persistence Road Garage — $7,600. Western Bellville, north of Vice City. The second-cheapest.
  • 7th Avenue Garage — $12,000. East of Vice City International Airport.
  • Store 'N' Bolt — $16,800. Northern Ambrosia, near the large monument.
  • Wyatt Motors — $21,600. Central Ambrosia, near the Pay 'N' Spray.
  • Vela Rota Drive Garage — $24,000. The middle of Capri.
  • Bonita Court Garage — $34,000. Center of Washington Island.
  • Atlantic Court Garage — $40,000. Ocean Beach, south of the Y Hotel. The most expensive.

A note on conflicting prices: one guide (TechWiser) lists Persistence Road at $9,500 and Vela Rota at $30,000. We use $7,600 and $24,000 because four other guides agree on them — and because those eight prices add up to exactly the $160,000 total that every guide agrees on for the Little Boxes trophy. If a price ever looks different in your game, the total is the check: the eight should sum to $160,000.

The 4 free story garages

These are automatic story rewards — you cannot buy them, and they do not count towards Little Boxes. Together they add eight more spaces (PowerPyx):

  • Port Gellhorn Station Garage — 1 space. Port Gellhorn, north of Moses' workshop and west of the Delights club. Unlocked by finishing Chapter 2, Daily Driver.
  • Thompson Court Garage — 2 spaces. Unlocked by finishing Chapter 4, Company Cars.
  • NE 15th Street Garage — 2 spaces. Stockyard (Wynwood) in Vice City. Unlocked by finishing Chapter 5, Operation Savage Glory.
  • Pegassi Residences Garage — 3 spaces. Unlocked by finishing Chapter 5, Operation Savage Glory.

That is 8 bought spaces + 8 story spaces = 16 garage spaces. Add Jason's and Lucia's two personal vehicles parked outside, and you reach the 18-vehicle maximum.

Ultimate Edition bonus: the Paradise Garage in northeast Watson Bay is free for Ultimate Edition owners — given by Wyman along with the '67 Dominator Buggy in Chapter 2. (TechWiser, GTA 6 Notebook) Our Ultimate Edition guide covers everything else that edition includes.

Limits and tips worth knowing

  • A full garage blocks registration. Registering a car needs an open slot, so if every space is taken you must sell a car or buy more space first. (TechWiser)
  • Parking an unowned car is not the same as owning it. If you have an empty garage space you can park an unregistered car there to keep it safe while it sits there — a useful stopgap until you can afford the registration fee. But the moment you take it out, it can still be permanently lost or destroyed. Only registration makes it recoverable. (SlashGear)
  • Safehouse parking helps early on. You also get two personal parking spaces outside your safehouse. Safehouses themselves cannot be bought or upgraded, and you get all four through the story: Jason's Safehouse in Key Lento (Chapter 1), Starlet Motel in Port Gellhorn (Chapter 2), Bayside Apartment in Vice City (Chapter 3) and Ocean Beach Home in Vice City (Chapter 4). Some garages also have weapon lockers for storing guns. (TechWiser)
  • Standard garages have limits. TechWiser reports they cannot hold vehicles with advanced trackers, and cannot hold military tanks — so do not plan your collection around either.
  • Swap cars from the garage or the app. Drive to a garage and switch a stored car yourself, or pay a fee in the Scooter Bros app to have one delivered to you. (GTA 6 Notebook)

FAQ

How do you store a car in a garage in GTA 6?
Register the car first, make sure you have a free garage slot, then send the car to the garage. A stolen car is not marked as owned until it is registered, so it cannot be stored safely before that. If your garages are full, sell a car or buy more space.

How many cars can you own in GTA 6?
Up to 18 vehicles at once: 16 garage spaces plus the two personal vehicles Jason and Lucia keep parked outside where they left them. The 16 spaces come from the 8 purchasable garages and the 4 story garages.

How do you unlock garages in GTA 6?
Meet Moses in Chapter 2, then finish the Chapter 2 mission Daily Driver. That installs the Vice City Real Estate app on your phone, from which you can buy garages, and it also unlocks the first free story garage in Port Gellhorn. More garages unlock automatically as you play through the story.
