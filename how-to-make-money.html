How to make money in GTA 6

Short answer: there is no Chapter 1 money farm worth your time, story missions, stick-ups and gang ops are all you have, and most Chapter 1 cash is wiped at the chapter's end anyway. The real money starts in Chapter 2 (sell stolen cars to Moses, about $1,000+ each), grows in Chapter 3 (jewelry heists up to $25,000), and peaks in Chapter 4 (bank vault robberies up to $205,000). Every method below is Reported from day-one player guides (checked 20 November 2026), amounts may vary or be patched, and Rockstar has published no official money guide.
Spoiler warning: this guide mentions story chapters and mission names from later in the game. If you are avoiding story details, finish the story first, these money methods will still be here.

Chapter 1: just enough to get by

  • Story missions are your main income. Cash is deliberately tight at the start.
  • Your first stolen-goods drop-off is Brian's lockup, unlocked in the Lab Fees mission, extra narcotics you find in the open world can be turned into cash there. (Reported)
  • Stick-ups and gang ops top it up, robberies and gang work are the only side earners this early.
  • Do not grind. Guides agree your wallet cash and floorboard stash are lost at the end of Chapter 1. Instead, put up to $5,000 into the Buck Me! Family Savings, that is the only money that carries over. Full story: what to do first in GTA 6.

Chapter 2: sell stolen cars to Moses

  • After the mission "Moses", Moses starts buying stolen cars, about $1,000 or more per car, with a short cooldown of roughly two minutes between sales. (Reported)
  • This is the first steady money loop in the game. A garage (buy one when you can) makes it far smoother.

Chapter 3: jewelry heists and the fences

  • Jewelry heists pay up to $25,000 a run. (Reported)
  • A story beat at the end of Chapter 3 pays $120,000 automatically. (Reported)
  • Know your fences: Yasmani buys jewelry and marked bills; Tyrone buys narcotics. Selling to the wrong fence is not an option, each takes specific goods. (Reported)

Chapter 4: bank vault robberies

  • Bank vault robberies pay up to $205,000, the biggest repeatable scores guides have found so far. (Reported)
  • They unlock after the optional mission "That's Where The Money Is…", follow the security van, and expect a 4-star wanted level on the way out. (Reported)
  • Yasmani sells security-van tips for $10,000, expensive, but they point you at the vans that lead to vault jobs. (Reported)

Small earners, any time

  • Muggings, small cash, everywhere.
  • Hunting, pristine animal skins sell to Bret and Killtelligence. (Reported)
  • Pawn-shop collectibles, hand in found collectibles for cash. (Reported)

One warning before you plan a fortune: every figure on this page comes from launch-week player guides (Polygon, PowerPyx, VGC, Game8), not from Rockstar. Payouts and cooldowns may differ in your copy or change with patches, this page is updated as players verify each method. New to the game? Start with what to do first, then come back here.

FAQ

What is the fastest way to make money in GTA 6?
It depends on your chapter. Chapter 2: sell stolen cars to Moses (about $1,000+ per car). Chapter 3: jewelry heists pay up to $25,000. Chapter 4: bank vault robberies pay up to $205,000. All figures are Reported from day-one player guides, checked 20 November 2026.

Can you farm money in Chapter 1 of GTA 6?
There is no real Chapter 1 farm, story missions, stick-ups and gang ops are the only earners, and player guides agree that wallet cash and your stash are lost at the end of Chapter 1 anyway. Put up to $5,000 into the Family Savings instead; that is the only money that carries over.

Who buys stolen cars in GTA 6?
Moses, starting in Chapter 2 after his mission. Guides report about $1,000 or more per car, with a short cooldown of roughly two minutes between sales.
