GTA 6 launch day checklist

GTA 6 unlocks on 19 November 2026. Work through this list the day before so launch night is playing, not downloading.

  • Free up storage — the download is up to 212 GB. Delete or archive games you are not playing until you have comfortably more than that free.
  • Start the preload — preload is already open on PS5 and Xbox. Start it now, not on launch night. Steps and a download-time calculator: preload guide.
  • Know your unlock time — PlayStation unlocks at midnight local; Xbox at 00:00 GMT worldwide. Find your city on the release times table.
  • Update your console — install any PS5 / Xbox system update today so it does not block you tomorrow.
  • Charge your controller — and plug in a headset if you use one. Simple, and everyone forgets.
  • Check your pre-order bonus — orders before 20 November include the Vintage Vice City Pack. Details: pre-order bonus page.
  • Expect a launch update — big launches usually ship a day-one patch. Leave time and bandwidth for it after the unlock before you judge performance.
  • Bookmark the mission guides — our mission guides are published one by one in story order from launch day.
Sources: Rockstar Store and storefront listings (preload, sizes, unlock timing). Checked 18 November 2026.
