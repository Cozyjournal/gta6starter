How GTA 6 cheats work

  • Finish the story first. Cheats cannot be used during the campaign. Once the credits roll, a Cheats list appears at the end of the Progression tab (open the Map, tab to Progression, scroll to the end).
  • Every cheat has its own unlock challenge — finish the story, win shooting-range medals, catch 100 fish, hold hands for four hours, and so on. When you complete a challenge, a pop-up tells you, and the requirement in the Cheats menu is replaced by that cheat's code.
  • Your story progress counts. Challenges track what you do while playing the story, so by the time you finish, several cheats may already be unlocked.
  • Four cheats unlock the moment the story ends: $250,000, Unlimited Focus, Attack Force and Cycle Weather.
  • Codes are D-pad sequences — identical on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S; only the shoulder-button names differ.

How to enter a cheat code

  • 1. Press Up on the D-pad to open your phone.
  • 2. Hold L1 (PS5) or LB (Xbox), then press R1 or RB. (Polygon, which tested on PS5, describes holding L1 + R1 together while you type the sequence.)
  • 3. Enter the cheat's D-pad sequence. The effect starts as soon as a correct code is entered for a cheat you have unlocked.

Typing a code for a cheat you have not unlocked yet does nothing — check the Cheats list in the Progress menu first.

Read this before you use one

  • Activating a cheat disables trophies and achievements and blocks saving for the rest of that play session.
  • Save manually first, have your fun, then quit and reload your clean save to get trophies and saving back.
  • Because saving is blocked, cash from the $250,000 cheat cannot be saved into your normal game — spend it in that session or lose it.
  • Entering codes before you finish the story does nothing, and you cannot start a new game with cheats on.

Where the lists differ

The major lists agree on almost everything above. Two small exceptions, so you know exactly what we printed and why: Cycle Weather — Dexerto prints the 8-input version shown above (ending Right, Right); KeenGamer's list ends one input earlier (…Up, Right). If one does not trigger, try the other. Raise Wanted Level — most lists print the 7-input version shown above; Dexerto's table shows an extra Up before the final Right, Up. Where outlets disagreed, we followed the majority list.

Fake lists — how to spot them

GTA 6 has no cheat phone numbers. If a "GTA 6 cheat codes" page shows phone numbers such as 1-999-7246-545-537 (PAINKILLER) or words like LAWYERUP, you are looking at GTA 5's codes with a GTA 6 label pasted on — dialling them in GTA 6 does nothing. The same goes for button lists copied from GTA 5 (for example "Right, X, Right, Left, Right, R1…"). Every real GTA 6 code is a D-pad-only sequence from the table above.

FAQ

Does GTA 6 have cheat codes?
Yes. GTA 6 has 61 cheat codes, published by Dexerto, Polygon, PC Gamer and GameSpot after hands-on time with the game. They only become usable after you finish the main story, and each one is unlocked by completing its own challenge.

How do you enter cheat codes in GTA 6?
Finish the story first. Then press Up on the D-pad to open your phone, hold L1 (PS5) or LB (Xbox) and press R1 or RB, then enter the cheat's D-pad sequence. A code only works if you have completed that cheat's unlock challenge.

Why is my GTA 6 cheat code not working?
Almost always one of three reasons: you have not finished the story yet, you have not completed that cheat's unlock challenge, or one press in the sequence was wrong — many codes share long openings and differ by a single input.

Is there a GTA 6 money cheat?
Yes — one. The $250,000 cheat unlocks when you finish the story; its code is Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right. It is the game's only money cheat, and because saving is blocked while a cheat is active, the cash cannot be carried into a normal save.

Do cheats disable trophies in GTA 6?
Yes. Activating any cheat disables trophies and achievements and blocks saving for the rest of that play session. Save manually before you enter a code, then reload your clean save when you are done.
