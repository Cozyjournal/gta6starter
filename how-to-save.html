How to save in GTA 6: autosave, manual saves and the greyed-out fix

Short answer: yes, GTA 6 autosaves, but only after missions, into one slot that keeps overwriting itself. It does not protect your free-roam progress, and players have already lost over an hour of play to a power cut because of that. Rockstar's update 01.002.002 (23 November 2026) fixes an issue that stopped the game autosaving correctly, install it, but keep saving by hand anyway. Manual saving is done from the pause menu (Game → Save Game) across 15 slots, but it stays greyed out during missions and through the whole prologue. If Save Game is greyed out for you, nothing is broken, you are just in one of those two situations. Full details below. (Checked 28 November 2026.)

Does GTA 6 autosave?

Yes. The game writes an autosave when you finish a mission, every launch guide agrees on that much. The catch is how thin that protection is:

  • One autosave slot only. Every new autosave replaces the last one. There is no second autosave to fall back on if something goes wrong. (Reported by Output Lag, via GameMapa and Nacional)
  • It cannot be switched off, there is no autosave setting in the menus. (Reported by Output Lag, via GameMapa)
  • Guides disagree about free roam. Game8 reports autosaves at key story moments and big purchases; Output Lag reports there is no autosave at all between missions, so an hour of side activities after your last mission is simply gone if the power cuts. Because the sources conflict, treat the safe version as true: between missions, assume nothing is saved until you save it.
  • Your autosave is hidden in Load Game. It does not appear in the save menu. Open Load Game and look for the entry labelled "(Autosave)" with the mission name and your completion percentage, plus its date and time, that timestamp tells you exactly how much progress an autosave actually holds.

Update 23 November 2026: Rockstar's update 01.002.002 fixes an issue that stopped the game autosaving correctly. Install it (PS5: highlight the game, press Options, choose Check for Update), but the one-slot limit and the free-roam gap above still apply, so keep saving manually. (Confirmed, Rockstar Support patch notes, via Moneycontrol and TechJuice.)

How to save manually

Unlike GTA 5, there is no quick save on the phone. Manual saving lives in the pause menu:

1. Press Options on PS5 or Menu on Xbox to pause.
2. Open the Game tab, it lists Save Game, Load Game and New Game.
3. Choose Save Game.
4. Pick an empty slot, or a used slot to overwrite it. The game asks you to confirm an overwrite, and the old save is then gone for good.
5. You get 15 manual slots. Each one shows the mission name, completion percentage, date and time, a picture and a short summary of where you are in the story. (Steps and slot details reported by GameMapa, Dot Esports and esports.gg)

Rotate your slots: keep one save before big story missions, one for free-roam sessions, and one spare. Because loading an older save means your next autosave overwrites the newer one, a spare slot is the only real undo button this game gives you.

Why is "Save Game" greyed out?

This is the question filling the forums this week, and the answer is almost never a bug. There are three normal causes:

  • You are still in the prologue. Save Game stays greyed out for the entire opening. Manual saving opens early in Chapter 1, Game Rant puts the moment at reaching Jason's house at the marina, usually after the first job for Brian. Until then you are living on autosaves, so play the opening in one sitting. (Reported by Game Rant, via GameMapa and Nacional)
  • You are in a mission. Saving is disabled mid-mission on purpose; the pause menu shows Retry Checkpoint and Restart Mission instead. Finish the mission and the autosave fires, then save manually as well. If you quit mid-mission, you return to your last save and the mission starts again from the beginning. (Reported by AllThingsHow and GTA Zone, via GameMapa and Nacional)
  • A cheat is active. Activating any cheat disables saving for that session, the same session block that disables trophies. Save to a spare slot before you enter a code. (Reported by Output Lag, via GameMapa; see our GTA 6 cheat codes guide for the full penalty list.)

How to load a save

Pause, open the Game tab and choose Load Game. The list shows your 15 manual saves and the autosave together, each with its mission name, percentage and time. Pick the autosave entry, labelled "(Autosave)", to return to your most recent mission checkpoint. One warning: loading an older manual save does not protect your newer autosave. The next autosave the game writes will overwrite it, so if the newer progress matters, save it manually first.

How not to lose progress: the habits that work

  • Save by hand before you quit, every time. Even straight after a mission. Side activities, purchases and exploration after that mission may not be in the autosave.
  • Trigger a mission beat after a long free-roam session if you want an autosave to fire, then save manually anyway.
  • Check the autosave timestamp in Load Game before assuming your progress is safe. If the timestamp is old, it is.
  • Back up before a crash-prone spot. If you are heading into a section where players report crashes, the prologue and the Pipework mission are the named ones, a fresh manual save costs you seconds. See our GTA 6 crashing fix for what is known about those.
  • Cloud saves help against console failure, not against overwrites. PS Plus and Xbox cloud saves protect the file itself; they do not stop the single autosave slot replacing your progress in-game.

FAQ

Does GTA 6 autosave?
Yes. GTA 6 autosaves after you complete a mission, into one dedicated autosave slot that overwrites itself each time. Launch guides disagree on whether it also saves at other moments between missions, so do not rely on it for free-roam progress, save manually before you quit.

Why is Save Game greyed out in GTA 6?
For two normal reasons: you are in the middle of a mission, or you are still in the prologue. Manual saving stays locked through the prologue and opens early in Chapter 1, Game Rant puts it at reaching Jason's house at the marina. Finish or leave the mission, or push through the opening, and the option lights up.

How many save slots does GTA 6 have?
15 manual save slots, plus one separate autosave slot that does not use any of the 15. Each manual slot shows the mission name, your completion percentage, the date and time, and a short summary.
