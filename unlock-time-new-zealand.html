Good to know in New Zealand

New Zealand is the first country on Earth to play GTA 6: when Auckland hits midnight, most of the world is still living on 18 November. That head start cuts both ways — Kiwi players will be posting opening-mission footage hours before anyone else, so if you are elsewhere and want to stay unspoiled, mute GTA 6 keywords on social media from the 18th. And New Zealand's midnight became the world's shortcut: because the Xbox unlock followed the console's region setting, around 2 million overseas players set their consoles to New Zealand and started at exactly the same moment as Kiwi players (Reported: Ampere Analysis via Polygon).
