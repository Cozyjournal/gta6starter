Good to know in New Zealand

New Zealand is the first country on Earth to play GTA 6: when Auckland hits midnight, most of the world is still living on 18 November. That head start cuts both ways — Kiwi players will be posting opening-mission footage hours before anyone else, so if you are elsewhere and want to stay unspoiled, mute GTA 6 keywords on social media from the 18th. One quirk for local Xbox players: because Xbox uses a single global unlock time, PlayStation is the early option in New Zealand by a full 13 hours.

Whichever platform you are on, the game can be downloaded ahead of launch: see the preload guide and download-time calculator to have it ready before unlock, and run through the launch day checklist so nothing is left to download at midnight.
