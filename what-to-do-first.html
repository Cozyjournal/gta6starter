GTA 6 — what to do first in Leonida

Short answer: play the Chapter 1 story missions first — they unlock almost everything else. And do not stop to farm money: day-one player guides agree that at the end of Chapter 1 you lose the safe house, your wallet cash and your stash. The only money that survives is what you put into the Buck Me! Family Savings (up to $5,000). Everything on this page is Reported from launch-day player guides (checked 20 November 2026) — Rockstar has published no official guide of its own.
Spoiler warning: this guide mentions story chapters and mission names from later in the game. If you are avoiding story details, finish the story first — these tips will still be here.

Do these first, in this order

  • Push the Chapter 1 story. Most systems stay locked until about halfway through Chapter 1 — before that you cannot sell stolen cars, fast travel, or take most side activities. The story is the unlock key for the whole game. (Reported)
  • Stash spare cash under the floorboards in Jason's bedroom. Stashed cash survives when you are wasted; cash in your wallet does not. (Reported)
  • Feed Jason's pet snake once a day — a small, free +3% Focus boost. (Reported)
  • Shoot hoops when you pass a court — +2% per basket, up to five baskets. (Reported)
  • Buy a suppressor at Ammu-Nation as soon as you can afford one — quiet stick-ups and stealth sections get much easier. (Reported)
  • Buy a garage when one becomes available — you will need somewhere to keep cars worth selling once the real money methods open up in Chapter 2. (Reported)

Easy side content around Key Lento

You start the game in Key Lento. Once the story lets you breathe, guides point to these low-pressure activities nearby (all Reported):

  • Kenton's treasure diving
  • Fishing with Cal
  • Shirley's requests
  • Robbery Opportunities — watch for the gold icons on the map
  • Running contraband for Donnie and Brian

The Chapter 1 trap — read this before grinding

Here is the thing most new players will learn the hard way: at the end of Chapter 1, the story takes Jason's safe house — and with it, according to multiple guides (Game8 and PowerPyx agree on this):

  • Lost: all money in your wallet, the whole floorboard stash, and Jason's fitness gains (they reset).
  • Kept: your Buck Me! Family Savings investments (up to $5,000), your relationship level with Lucia, and unique weapons.

FAQ

What should I do first in GTA 6?
Play the Chapter 1 story missions first. They unlock most of the game's systems — you cannot sell stolen cars, fast travel or take most side activities until about halfway through Chapter 1. (Reported by day-one player guides, checked 20 November 2026.)

Do you lose your money at the end of Chapter 1 in GTA 6?
Yes, according to multiple player guides: at the end of Chapter 1 you lose the safe house, all wallet money and the floorboard stash, and Jason's fitness resets. Only Family Savings investments (up to $5,000), your relationship level with Lucia and unique weapons carry over.

Where do you stash cash in GTA 6?
Under the floorboards in Jason's bedroom. Stashed cash survives when your character is wasted; cash carried in your wallet does not. (Reported.)
