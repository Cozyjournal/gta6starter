GTA 6 controls — sprint moved, and how to get the old layout back

Short answer: the biggest change in 25 years of GTA — sprint is now clicking the left stick (L3 on PS5, LS on Xbox), not tapping X/A. If your muscle memory rebels, GTA 6 includes a Classic control scheme that restores the GTA 5 layout. Full layout below, from launch-day reporting (checked 19 November 2026); Rockstar has not published an official controls table yet, so treat the full list as Reported.

The headline change

  • Sprint: L3 / LS (click the left stick) — Confirmed by multiple launch-day reports. Tapping X/A no longer sprints on the default scheme.
  • Want the GTA 5 layout back? Open Settings → Control Scheme tab and cycle to "Classic". That restores: sprint on Cross/A, melee on Circle/B, jump & climb on Square/X, crouch on L3/LS.

Default layout (Reported)

  • Sprint — L3 (PS5) / LS (Xbox)
  • Crouch — Circle (PS5) / B (Xbox)
  • Melee — Square (PS5) / X (Xbox)
  • Jump — Cross (PS5) / A (Xbox)
  • Handbrake — Cross (PS5) / A (Xbox), while driving
  • Interact — L1 (PS5) / LB (Xbox)

This table comes from preview and launch-day reporting (SlashGear, Spreely and others, 18–19 November 2026). If you find a button doing something different in your copy, tell us — this page is corrected as players verify each input.

Tips for the first hour

  • Give the new sprint one full mission before switching — most players adapt within the opening sequence.
  • If you switch to Classic, do it before your first long session, not after — relearning twice is worse than relearning once.
  • Pair this with our best settings guide — subtitles on and Game Mode on your TV make the opening far easier to follow.

FAQ

How do you sprint in GTA 6?
Click the left stick — L3 on PS5, LS on Xbox. On the default control scheme, tapping X/A no longer sprints.

Can I change GTA 6 controls back to GTA 5 style?
Yes. Settings → Control Scheme tab → choose "Classic". Sprint returns to Cross/A, melee to Circle/B, jump/climb to Square/X and crouch to L3/LS.
