GTA 6 release date and unlock times

GTA 6 releases on Thursday, 19 November 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and unlocks at midnight local time in most store regions. Confirmed

When it unlocks where you live

The launch rolls around the world: each store region unlocks at its own midnight on 19 November, so New Zealand plays first. Your unlock time is simply 12:00 AM on 19 November, your local time (PlayStation Store regions; Xbox unlocks at 00:00 GMT in most regions).

The road to 19 November

  • December 2023: first trailer points to a 2025 release.
  • 2 May 2025: Rockstar delays the game to 26 May 2026.
  • 6 November 2025: second delay — moved to 19 November 2026.
  • 25 June 2026: pre-orders open worldwide.
  • 10 November 2026: preload opens (moved forward from 12 November).
  • 19 November 2026: launch day.

The game has gone gold, so a further delay is very unlikely.

Platforms

GTA 6 launches on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. There is no PS4 or Xbox One version, and no PC version at launch.
