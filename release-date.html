When it unlocks where you live

The launch rolled around the world: each store region unlocked at its own midnight on 19 November, on both platforms, so New Zealand played first. The table below shows that local-midnight moment — PlayStation and Xbox unlocked together in every region. One twist: on Xbox the unlock followed the console's region setting, so around 2 million players set their consoles to New Zealand and started at New Zealand's midnight instead (Reported: Ampere Analysis via Polygon).

The road to 19 November

  • December 2023: first trailer points to a 2025 release.
  • 2 May 2025: Rockstar delays the game to 26 May 2026.
  • 6 November 2025: second delay — moved to 19 November 2026.
  • 25 June 2026: pre-orders open worldwide.
  • 10 November 2026: preload opens (moved forward from 12 November).
  • 19 November 2026: launch day.

The game has gone gold before launch, and it released on schedule on 19 November 2026.

Platforms

GTA 6 launches on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. There is no PS4 or Xbox One version, and no PC version at launch.

Frequently asked questions

What day does GTA 6 come out?
Thursday, 19 November 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
What time does GTA 6 unlock?
At midnight local time (12:00 AM on 19 November) in most PlayStation Store regions. Xbox unlocked at the same local midnight as PlayStation in each region.
Which country can play GTA 6 first?
New Zealand — it unlocks at 12:00 AM local time there, which is 4:00 PM on 18 November in Pakistan (PKT).
Is GTA 6 on PS4, Xbox One or PC at launch?
No. GTA 6 launched only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and there is no PC version at launch.
