Quick answers

When does GTA 6 come out?
Thursday 19 November 2026, worldwide, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It unlocks at midnight local time in most store regions.
Is GTA 6 on PS4 or Xbox One?
No. GTA 6 is only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S at launch.
Is GTA 6 coming to PC?
Not at launch. Rockstar has not announced a PC version; a trade-press report points to early 2027. Treat any site selling a PC pre-order as a scam.
How much storage do I need?
Up to 212 GB of free space for the preload, depending on console.
Does the boxed copy have a disc?
No — boxed copies are code-in-box: the box holds a download code, so you download the full game either way.
