Pre-order bonus — deadline

Orders placed before 20 November 2026 include the Vintage Vice City Pack bonus. After that date, the bonus is gone.

Which edition should you buy?

For most players: Standard. The game itself is identical. Choose Ultimate only if you value the extra digital items. Skip the Collector's box unless you collect — and remember it does not include the game.

Boxed copies are code-in-box: there is no playable disc. You will download up to 212 GB either way — see the preload guide.
