Pre-order bonus — deadline

Orders placed before 20 November 2026 include the Vintage Vice City Pack bonus. After that date, the bonus is gone.

Black Friday 2026 — no official discount

There is no official Black Friday discount on GTA 6: the PlayStation Store and Xbox Store still list it at $79.99 / $99.99, and neither Sony's sale (to 30 November) nor Microsoft's (to 2 December) includes it. Take-Two has said it will not cut the price for the holidays. Some UK retailers sell boxed codes and keys below the £69.99 RRP — the cheapest reported is £53.85 for Xbox digital — but those are retailer prices, not Rockstar's. (Reported by Hudwire, GamingBible and 6cheats, checked 25 November 2026.)

Which edition should you buy?

For most players: Standard. The game itself is identical. Choose Ultimate only if you value the extra digital items. Skip the Collector's box unless you collect — and remember it does not include the game.

Boxed copies are code-in-box: there is no playable disc. You will download up to 212 GB either way — see the preload guide.

Choosing between the two real editions? The Ultimate Edition guide breaks down exactly what the extra $20 buys — including the Ultimate-only Rideout Customs car shop.

Frequently asked questions

How much is GTA 6 in the US?
GTA 6 costs $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition in the US.
How much is GTA 6 in India?
In India, GTA 6 costs ₹5,999 for the Standard Edition and ₹7,499 for the Ultimate Edition.
Does the Collector's Edition include the game?
No. The $399.99 Collector's box (The Goodtime State – Vice City Collection) contains physical collector's items only — the game is NOT included and must be bought separately.
When does the pre-order bonus end?
Orders placed before 20 November 2026 included the Vintage Vice City Pack pre-order bonus. That offer has now ended.
Is GTA 6 on Game Pass or PS Plus?
No. Rockstar has not announced GTA 6 for Game Pass or PlayStation Plus — it is a full-price purchase on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Not announced
