How to preload GTA 6

Preload opened on 10 November 2026. If you pre-ordered digitally, you can download GTA 6 now — up to 212 GB — so it unlocks the moment your region hits midnight on 19 November. Confirmed

Launch-night checklist

  • Console ready — a PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S. There is no PC, PS4 or Xbox One version at launch.
  • Storage cleared — free up to 212 GB (steps below).
  • Copy secured — pre-ordered, or a launch-day plan. Boxed copies are code-in-box and still need the full download.
  • Download started early — on a slow connection the preload can take a full day. Use the calculator below.

Preload on PS5

  1. Open the PlayStation Store and find your GTA 6 pre-order (or open it from your Library).
  2. Select Download. Preload opened at local midnight on 10 November.
  3. Keep the console in rest mode with downloads enabled so it finishes in the background.

Preload on Xbox Series X|S

  1. Open the Microsoft Store or the Xbox app and find your GTA 6 pre-order.
  2. Select Pre-install. Xbox preload opened at 00:00 GMT on 10 November.
  3. You can also start it remotely from the Xbox mobile app if the game is on your wishlist/order.

How to free 212 GB

  • Delete games you have finished — you can always re-download them later.
  • Move PS4/Xbox One games to an external USB drive (they play from there; PS5/Xbox Series titles must stay on internal or expansion storage).
  • Clear captured screenshots and video clips you no longer need.
  • Check the real free space in Settings → Storage before you start, not after the download fails.
