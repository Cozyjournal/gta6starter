One honest note before you start: these are the standard PlayStation and Xbox download fixes, not GTA 6-specific repairs. If the problem is on the network's side during launch week, no setting on your console will fix it — you just have to wait and retry.

The fixes, in order

  1. Check your free storage first. GTA 6 needs up to 212 GB. If space runs out mid-download, the console pauses it without much explanation. Delete or move games you are not playing, then resume. Check the real number in Settings → Storage.
  2. Pause and resume the download. A stuck progress bar often restarts cleanly after a pause of 30 seconds.
  3. Restart the console fully. A complete power-off and restart (not rest mode / not quick resume) clears stuck download queues on both platforms.
  4. Check the service status pages. Look at the PlayStation Network and Xbox Live status pages. Launch-week congestion is normal for a release this size, and a yellow or red service means the fix is patience, not settings.
  5. PS5: restore licences. Go to Settings → Users and Accounts → Other → Restore Licenses, then retry the download. This fixes most "can't download" cases where the console has lost track of your pre-order.
  6. Improve the connection. Switch to a wired (Ethernet) connection if you can, or move the console closer to the router. A 212 GB download on weak Wi-Fi can stall repeatedly.
  7. Cancel and restart the download. Delete the partial download and start again from your Library or the store page. Your purchase is the licence on your account, so re-downloading is always free — you lose nothing but time.
  8. Update the console software. Make sure the PS5 or Xbox system software is fully up to date, then try again. Outdated system software can block new downloads from starting.

Still stuck?

Run through the launch day checklist to make sure nothing else is missing, and use the download-time calculator on our preload page to check whether your connection can realistically finish 212 GB before the 19 November unlock.
