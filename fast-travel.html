How to fast travel in GTA 6

Short answer: GTA 6 has no click-on-the-map teleport. To fast travel, you ride there instead: call Paradise Taxi from your phone, book a RydeMe through its app, or catch a bus or train. Once you are in the vehicle, hold L2/LT to focus on the driver and press Triangle/Y to skip the journey — the skip costs a flat $100 on top of the fare, whatever the distance. Everything below is Reported from launch-week guides (checked 23 November 2026); Rockstar has published no official guide of its own.

Fast travel methods at a glance

  • Paradise Taxi — available after Gone Fishing in Chapter 1. Call from your phone contacts. Pays with the cash you are carrying. (Reported)
  • RydeMe — the rideshare app. Unlocks after the Buck Me mission gives you a bank account. Pays from your bank account, not your pocket. (Reported)
  • Bus and train — fixed routes from stops and stations. Cheapest option for places near a route. (Reported)
  • Skip the ride — an extra $100 on any taxi or RydeMe trip, whatever the distance. (Reported)

How to fast travel with Paradise Taxi

Paradise Taxi is your only fast travel option until RydeMe unlocks, and it stays useful whenever RydeMe has no driver available. It works much like GTA 5's Downtown Cab Co. The in-game help also mentions hailing a cab on the street, but calling from your phone is the reliable way: (Reported)

  1. Place a waypoint on the world map at your destination. You can pick an area you have not explored yet — the taxi can take you to fogged, undiscovered areas. (Reported)
  2. Open your phone by pressing Up on the d-pad.
  3. Go to Contacts > All Contacts > Paradise Taxi and call. The dispatcher confirms a taxi is on its way, as long as you are somewhere accessible near a road.
  4. Look for a white car icon on the minimap. If you don't see it, press Down on the d-pad to bring the minimap up.
  5. When the taxi arrives, get in and select your destination.
  6. Hold L2 on PS5 or LT on Xbox to focus on the driver, then press Triangle/Y to skip the ride and fast travel. Skipping costs an extra $100 on top of the regular fare.

If you are short on cash, just stay in the taxi and let the driver take you there in real time — the ride itself still gets you there without driving. Taxis cannot reach isolated spots like the remote keys or deep in the swamps, so for those you will still have to drive yourself. See the Leonida map to plan where a taxi can and cannot go. (Reported)

How to fast travel with RydeMe

RydeMe is GTA 6's rideshare app — the most convenient way to travel once it is unlocked, because you book from your phone without calling anyone. Before you can use it, you need a bank account, which you get in the Chapter 1 mission Buck Me: after Fleeca Bank turns you down for bad credit, you set an account up on your phone instead. Once your account is active, the RydeMe app appears on your phone automatically — you don't download anything. Some walkthroughs place the app in Chapter 2, so if it has not appeared right after Buck Me, keep playing the story missions. (Reported)

  1. Open your phone (Up on the d-pad) and select the RydeMe app.
  2. Pick your destination — your safehouse, an existing map marker, or a new destination — and pay the displayed fare. The further the trip, the higher the fare.
  3. Wait for your driver. Their vehicle shows as a white car icon on the minimap.
  4. Get in. During the trip you can listen to music, browse your phone, or chat with your driver or other passengers.
  5. To skip the ride, hold L2/LT and press Triangle/Y. This costs an extra $100.

Important: RydeMe charges your BuckMe bank account, not your pocket cash, so make sure you have enough in the account before booking. If no RydeMe driver is available for your destination, call Paradise Taxi instead. (Reported)

How much does fast travel cost?

Every trip has two parts: the ride fare and the optional skip fee. The fare depends on the journey — RydeMe fares grow with distance, so crossing Leonida costs more than a short trip inside Vice City — but the skip fee is always a flat $100, whatever the distance. Taxis take the cash you are carrying; RydeMe charges your bank account. The $100 skip feels expensive early on, but as you earn more it becomes a cheap way to cross the map. Our money guide covers the best early ways to earn. (Reported)

One extra saving, reported by KeenGamer: in Chapter 3 you can unlock a BuckMe Premium Account, which gives a 20% discount on RydeMe journeys. Game8 also reports a cheaper Hurry option — $5 each — that prompts the driver to go faster in real time instead of skipping outright. (Reported, single source each)

How to fast travel by bus and train

Leonida also has public transport. Buses and trains run fixed routes, so you have to go to a stop or a station — you can't summon one to your location. Hold Down on the d-pad to find nearby stops and stations, travel to one, and check when the next service arrives. Board when it comes, choose an available destination along its route, and hold Triangle/Y to skip the journey instead of riding in real time. It is useful when your destination sits near a route, but taxis and RydeMe are more convenient because they take you to a specific place without waiting for a scheduled service. (Reported — KeenGamer is the only guide with full bus and train steps so far)

Tips and problems

  • No driver available? If RydeMe finds nobody, call Paradise Taxi from your contacts instead. (Reported)
  • Ride cancelled? Rides cancel automatically if you request one from an off-road or inaccessible area — move near a road and try again. (Reported, Game8)
  • Don't attack your driver. Attacking a RydeMe driver temporarily disables RydeMe bookings. (Reported, Game8)
  • Free option: you can also let Jason or Lucia drive themselves to your waypoint for free with auto-drive, if you'd rather not pay a fare at all. (Reported, GTABase)
  • Character-switch trick: Game8 reports you can passively travel without paying by switching between Jason and Lucia while driving, which puts your current character in the passenger seat. (Reported, single source)
  • Don't skip everything. Fast travel skips the places and random encounters you would find by driving, so explore a new area yourself at least once, then use a taxi or RydeMe when you need to get back quickly. (Reported)

FAQ

Can you fast travel in GTA 6?
Yes, but there is no map teleport. You ride there instead: call Paradise Taxi, book a RydeMe, or take a bus or train, then pay $100 to skip the journey. (Reported)

How do you unlock fast travel in GTA 6?
Paradise Taxi is available after the Gone Fishing mission in Chapter 1. RydeMe unlocks after the Buck Me mission gives you a bank account. (Reported)

How much does fast travel cost in GTA 6?
You pay the ride fare, which grows with distance, plus a flat $100 to skip the journey. Taxis take the cash you are carrying; RydeMe charges your bank account. (Reported)

Why is there no RydeMe app on my phone in GTA 6?
Finish the Buck Me mission first so you have a bank account. Some walkthroughs place the app in Chapter 2, so if it has not appeared, keep playing the story missions. (Reported)

Can you fast travel to undiscovered areas in GTA 6?
Yes. Taxis and RydeMe can take you to fogged, undiscovered areas on the map. They cannot reach isolated spots like the remote keys or deep in the swamps. (Reported)
