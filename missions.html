GTA 6 mission guides

Mission guides are live — one mission at a time, in story order. Each guide is written from verified gameplay information and checked before it is published. No invented walkthroughs, ever.

What every mission guide will include

  • Mission facts — name, who gives it, where it starts, what it unlocks, and the reward.
  • Objectives — the full objective list, in order.
  • Step-by-step walkthrough — what to do at each stage, in plain language.
  • Fail conditions — what makes the mission fail, so you can avoid restarting.
  • Tips — the easiest way through the hard parts.
  • Previous / next mission — so you can follow the story in order.
