How missions work in GTA 6

  • No countdown on story missions — story missions have no timer, and the gold-medal time objectives from older games are gone. Take them at your own pace. Reported
  • Some optional missions can expire — an hourglass icon marks a time-sensitive side mission. It can be missed, but missing one does not block trophies or 100% completion. Reported
  • No mission replay — a finished mission cannot be replayed from a menu, so save before a mission you want to try differently. Reported
  • Order can vary a little — some missions open at the same time (Lab Fees and Just A Scratch open together with Gear Up), so your order may differ slightly from this list.
  • Saving matters — manual saving unlocks at Jason's marina house after the prologue; see how to save in GTA 6.
  • How long is the story? — see GTA 6 intro and story length for the verified numbers so far.

FAQ

Can you replay missions in GTA 6?

No. Based on launch coverage, GTA 6 has no mission-replay option — a finished mission cannot be replayed from a menu. Reported

Do GTA 6 missions have time limits?

Story missions have no countdown timer, and the old gold-medal time objectives are gone. Some optional missions are time-sensitive: an hourglass icon means the mission can expire and be missed, but missing one does not block trophies or 100% completion. Reported

How many missions are in GTA 6?

Not announced. Rockstar has not published a mission count, so this hub lists guides in verified story order instead of guessing a total. Not announced
