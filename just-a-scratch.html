Just a Scratch: fix the Ganado for free at Joey's garage

Spoiler warning: this guide describes one Chapter 1 story mission, a free garage visit, not a shootout. Short answer: Just a Scratch sends Jason to Coastal Auto Care in Key Lento, where the mechanic Joey fixes up his battered '70 Ganado. The engine repair and the respray are free during this mission only, pick Full Engine > Stock Engine (Restore), then Cosmetics > Respray and choose any gloss colour. You also get Joey as a phone contact for tows and deliveries, and a small boost to Jason and Lucia's relationship. Everything below is Reported from launch-week walkthroughs (checked 25 November 2026); Rockstar has published no official guide of its own.

Mission facts

  • Chapter: Chapter 1, mandatory story mission, needed for 100% completion. (Reported)
  • Mission number: trackers disagree, PowerPyx and VGC call it the 5th main mission, GTABase lists it 7th, and the in-game Story Recap places it 4th. The number does not matter for playing it. (Reported)
  • Map objective: "Fix Your Car", the marker carries a pink car and a heart, and the card notes "Lucia will appreciate this". (Reported)
  • Given by: Joey, the mechanic at Coastal Auto Care in Key Lento. Cal told Joey the car is in bad shape, and Joey texts Jason with the shop's pin. (Reported)
  • Unlock: finish Gone Fishing. Just a Scratch opens at the same time as Gear Up, Lab Fees and Unwanted Attention, so your own order may differ from the trackers'. (Reported)
  • Where: Coastal Auto Care, on the main road through Key Lento, a couple of minutes from Jason's stilt house, a whitewashed block with a red canopy and a used-car lot. (Reported)
  • You play: Jason. (Reported)
  • Reward: free engine repair and free respray for the Ganado; Joey added to Jason's contacts; a relationship-level increase between Jason and Lucia. (Reported)

How to start Just a Scratch

After Gone Fishing, take Jason's truck out and wait for a text from Joey, it can arrive wherever you are, sometimes seconds after you leave Jason's house. The text carries a pin for Coastal Auto Care, and the "Fix Your Car" objective appears on the map in the south part of Key Lento. Set it as your destination and drive the '70 Ganado there. The Ganado is Jason's only car in Chapter 1 and it shows: rust everywhere, a primer-black front clip, and an engine Joey can hear knocking from down the street, worn rod bearings in its 7-litre V8. (Reported)

If the Ganado is wrecked: make a manual save, then reload it from the pause menu (Game > Save/Load Game). You respawn at Jason's house with the car parked outside. See our how to save in GTA 6 guide if you have not unlocked manual saving yet. (Reported)

Step by step

  1. Wait for Joey's text after Gone Fishing, then drive the '70 Ganado to Coastal Auto Care in Key Lento.
  2. Talk to Joey outside the garage, hold Up on the D-pad to face him, then press X on PlayStation or A on Xbox to greet him.
  3. Drive the Ganado slowly into the garage when the shutter opens.
  4. Select Full Engine, then Stock Engine (Restore), free during this mission.
  5. Select Cosmetics > Respray and pick any gloss colour you like, also free.
  6. Leave the shop to trigger the closing cutscene with Joey and finish the mission.

Talk to Joey: the free repair deal

Joey waits outside under the red canopy next to the teal roller door. Hold Up on the D-pad to look at him, then press the prompt to greet him. One look at the bodywork and one listen to the engine tells him the problem, rod bearings, and he makes Jason a deal: the repair is on him this time, as long as Jason brings his business back to Coastal Auto Care next time the car needs fixing. If you arrive without the Ganado, a short cutscene plays showing Jason returning with the car. (Reported)

Restore the engine: Full Engine, free this once

Once the Ganado is inside, the repair menu opens. Select Full Engine at the top of the menu, then choose Stock Engine (Restore). The menu lists the restore at $100, but the mission covers it, you pay nothing. This is the step players fumble, so take it slowly: the free repair is the whole point of the mission, and it is also your first tour of the mod-shop system. (Reported)

Respray the Ganado: any gloss colour, free

From the main menu, scroll to the Cosmetics menu and select Respray. Scroll through the colours and pick any you like with the gloss finish, it is free during this mission. The solid metallic versions of each colour are not free, so skip those unless you want to pay. Once you have picked, Joey invites you to look at other customisation options, but those cost money, and this early in the game you probably cannot afford much anyway, so it is best to call the repairs there and leave. (Reported)

Ultimate Edition players: IGN reports you can skip the basic work and upgrade the Ganado to the free Retro Build included with the Ultimate Edition instead. That detail rests on IGN alone, so treat it as single-source Reported. (Reported)

What Just a Scratch unlocks

Joey becomes a standing contact. You can call him to have the Ganado repaired or delivered, and later he offers a tow you can request by phone when a car needs recovering out in the world. The mission also raises Jason and Lucia's relationship level, the "Fix Your Car" card says Lucia will appreciate it, and fixing the car before her release is the point of the errand. Later visits to Coastal Auto Care cost money, and you will need the car in full repair before you can buy a new paint job or modifications there. For the wider picture on spending, see our car customisation guide. (Reported)

What can go wrong

  • Don't buy upgrades for the Ganado. The story replaces this car later in Chapter 1 and upgrades on it are lost, save your money for the next car. (Reported)
  • You can't register a stolen car yet. The ability to register a new personal vehicle unlocks automatically in Chapter 2 from story progress, so don't expect to swap the Ganado for something stolen off the street. (Reported)
  • Don't pay for the engine restore. If the menu shows $100, that is the list price, during this mission the repair is free. Pick Stock Engine (Restore) and confirm without spending. (Reported)
  • No special fail condition is listed in the walkthroughs we checked for this mission, it is one of the shortest missions in the game, and it works like a tutorial for repairing a car. If you die or get arrested, the mission restarts from a checkpoint, as in other missions. (Reported)

Previous and next missions

Just a Scratch is one of the four Key Lento errands that open after Gone Fishing, alongside Gear Up, Lab Fees and Unwanted Attention, play them in any order. Once they are done, Pipework unlocks. See the full list on our GTA 6 missions page. (Reported)

FAQ

How do you start Just a Scratch in GTA 6?
Finish Gone Fishing, then wait for a text from Joey the mechanic. The "Fix Your Car" objective appears on the map in south Key Lento, drive Jason's '70 Ganado to Coastal Auto Care. (Reported)

Is the repair in Just a Scratch really free?
Yes. The engine repair and the respray are free during this mission only. The menu shows the engine restore at $100, but the mission covers it; later visits to the shop cost money. (Reported)

What do you get for finishing Just a Scratch?
A free engine repair and a free respray for the Ganado, Joey added to Jason's contacts for tow and delivery calls, and a small boost to Jason and Lucia's relationship. (Reported)

What if I destroyed the Ganado before Just a Scratch?
Make a manual save, then reload it from the pause menu (Game > Save/Load Game). You respawn at Jason's house with the Ganado parked outside. (Reported)

Should I buy upgrades for the Ganado in Just a Scratch?
No. The story replaces the Ganado later in Chapter 1, and upgrades on it are lost, save your money. You also cannot register a stolen car as your personal vehicle until Chapter 2. (Reported)
