GTA 6 Ultimate Edition: is it worth the extra $20?

The Ultimate Edition costs $99.99 — $20 more than Standard ($79.99) — and adds extra digital content on top of the same game. The game itself is identical in both editions. Confirmed

Who should buy Ultimate?

Buy Ultimate if you already know you will spend serious time in Leonida and you enjoy extra digital items. Buy Standard if you just want the game — you lose nothing from the story or the world by saving the $20.

Collector's Edition warning

The $399.99 "Goodtime State – Vice City Collection" is a box of physical collector's items. It does not include the game. You would still need to buy GTA 6 separately.
