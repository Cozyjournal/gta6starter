Lab Fees: collect Brian's rent and stash the acid

Spoiler warning: this guide describes one Chapter 1 story mission — a rent collection that turns into a drug pickup. Short answer: Lab Fees sends Jason to Moonstone Boutique in Key Lento to collect Brian's rent. The clerk has no money, so Jason takes acid from the lab behind the shop instead — lab door code 0420 — then delivers it to Brian's lockup at the southern end of the Keys — lockup code 7700. The reward is $600, and the mission unlocks your first stolen-goods drop-off. Everything below is Reported from launch-week walkthroughs (checked 24 November 2026); Rockstar has published no official guide of its own.

Mission facts

  • Chapter: Chapter 1 — mandatory story mission, needed for 100% completion. (Reported)
  • Mission number: the 4th main mission, according to PowerPyx. Trackers disagree on exact numbering, because Lab Fees, Gear Up and Just a Scratch all open at the same time after Gone Fishing and can be played in different orders. (Reported)
  • Given by: Brian Heder. (Reported)
  • Unlock: finish Gone Fishing, then select the "Collect Brian's Rent" objective on the map. (Reported)
  • Where: Moonstone Boutique, a gift shop in the artist village on the south-west side of Key Lento, behind Wonder Whale. (Reported)
  • You play: Jason. (Reported)
  • Reward: $600. Since Jason has no bank account yet, the cash arrives later in the mailbox in front of his house. (Reported)
  • Unlocks: Brian's lockup as a stolen-goods drop-off, and your introduction to the Criminal Profile. (Reported)

How to start Lab Fees

Open the map and select the objective "Collect Brian's Rent", then drive to Key Lento. Moonstone Boutique sits in a small artist village full of shops, behind Wonder Whale, the local gift shop, near the picnic tables. When you reach the boutique you will find the door locked — take out your phone (tap D-pad Up; holding it opens the WAiNK app instead), open the green Contacts app and call Brian. He tells you the boutique is really an acid lab and that he wants his money, collected however you like. Go in when you are ready — the mission name appears in pink at the bottom right of the screen once you are inside. (Reported)

Free collectible on the way in: as you approach past Wonder Whale, you get a notification that a WAiNK scan is nearby. Hold D-pad Up to open the WAiNK app, walk to the huge whale sculpture in front of Wonder Whale — a flower symbol hangs over it even when it is out of your line of sight — and press R1/RB to scan it. The sculpture is logged in your Collections. (Reported)

Step by step

  1. Select "Collect Brian's Rent" on the map and drive to Moonstone Boutique in Key Lento.
  2. Call Brian from your phone at the locked shop door, then go inside.
  3. Talk to the clerk behind the counter and get him to hand over payment.
  4. Enter the lab behind the shop with door code 0420 and collect the acid.
  5. Call Brian again — he tells you to drop the acid at his lockup further south.
  6. Drive to Brian's lockup at the Isla Tropical private club, enter code 7700, and stash the acid in the strongbox.

Before any mission like this, a manual save is cheap insurance — GTA 6 has no mission replay (Reported).

Talk to the clerk: Defuse or Threaten

The shop is bright, colourful and completely empty of customers. A tip about the Criminal Profile appears as you enter — you can turn these tips off in the UI tab of Settings if you prefer. Hold L2/LT and speak to the clerk. Jason asks for the rent and the clerk says he doesn't have it — rent, he insists, is a social construct — and it soon becomes clear he would not pay anyway. You then get a choice, and a third way to settle it is open to you as well. (Reported)

  • Defuse: try to talk him down. He brushes you off again, then pulls out a baseball bat — grab it from him (Triangle/Y) and he gets scared immediately. (Reported)
  • Threaten: point your gun at him until he gives in. (Reported)
  • Punch him once: one punch is enough — he offers the acid in the back room as payment instead of cash. (Reported)

This is your introduction to the Criminal Profile. How you handle criminal situations — polite and non-lethal, or violent — shifts your profile, and this choice is one of the first moments that counts. Non-lethal, polite handling builds a Professional profile; needless killing or attacking civilians erodes it. (Reported)

Don't kill the clerk. The 0420 code still works if you do, but killing him alerts the police. There is no need — any of the three approaches above gets you the code. (Reported)

Enter the lab: door code 0420

Walk behind the counter to the locked door in the corner and interact with the keypad (Triangle/Y). The code is 0420 — the clerk tells you it during the confrontation, and you can also find it written on a notepad inside the shop. Inside, pick up the acid, and grab the cash in the safebox in the same room before you leave. You can loot the cash register on your way out too. (Reported)

Call Brian and stash the acid: lockup code 7700

Leave the boutique and call Brian again. He tells you to deliver the narcotics to his lockup at the southern end of the Leonida Keys. Follow the green marker to the Isla Tropical private club and look for the fish mural near the parking area — that marks the lockup entrance. Interact with the keypad beside the door and enter 7700. Inside, approach the strongbox beside the red tool chest and press Triangle/Y when the "Deposit Narcotics" prompt appears. Depositing the goods completes Lab Fees. (Reported)

What Lab Fees unlocks

Brian's lockup becomes your first stolen-goods drop-off. If you find extra narcotics while wandering the open world or exploring, this stash is where you can turn them into cash — selling stolen goods to fences such as Brian is one of the early money loops. See our how to make money in GTA 6 guide for the full picture. (Reported)

What can go wrong

  • Killing the clerk alerts the police — use Defuse, Threaten or one punch instead. (Reported)
  • Don't skip the phone call. The boutique door stays locked until you call Brian from outside; if you can't get in, stand near the entrance, press D-pad Up and call him through Contacts. (Reported)
  • No special fail condition is listed in the walkthroughs we checked for this mission — the clerk confrontation can't fail it. If you die or get arrested, the mission restarts from a checkpoint, as in other missions. (Reported)

Previous and next missions

Lab Fees unlocks after Gone Fishing, at the same time as Gear Up and Just a Scratch — so your own order may differ from the trackers'. Just a Scratch is the other early Key Lento errand, and our walkthrough for it is live. See the full list on our GTA 6 missions page. (Reported)

FAQ

What is the Lab Fees lab door code in GTA 6?
0420. The clerk tells you the code during the confrontation, and it is also written on a notepad in the shop. (Reported)

What is Brian's lockup code in GTA 6?
7700. Enter it on the keypad at Brian's lockup by the fish mural at the Isla Tropical private club, at the southern end of the Leonida Keys. (Reported)

How do you start Lab Fees in GTA 6?
Finish Gone Fishing, then select the "Collect Brian's Rent" objective on the map and drive to Moonstone Boutique in Key Lento. Call Brian from your phone at the locked shop door, then go in. (Reported)

What is the reward for Lab Fees in GTA 6?
$600. Since Jason has no bank account yet, the cash arrives later in the mailbox in front of his house. (Reported)

What does Lab Fees unlock in GTA 6?
Brian's lockup becomes your first stolen-goods drop-off, where you can turn extra narcotics and other stolen goods into cash, and the mission introduces the Criminal Profile. (Reported)
