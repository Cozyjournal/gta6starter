GTA 6 unlocks in the UK at 12:00 AM local time on Thursday, 19 November 2026 — on both platforms. PlayStation unlocks at midnight local time, and Xbox unlocks at 00:00 GMT worldwide; because London is on GMT in November, those two moments are exactly the same: 12:00 AM, 19 November. Confirmed

Good to know in the UK

The UK is the one country where the platform debate disappears: Xbox's worldwide unlock time is set to GMT, and British clocks are on GMT in November, so PlayStation and Xbox players in the UK start at the identical second. British players do start five hours behind Pakistan and a full half-day behind New Zealand, which makes the UK launch morning the peak spoiler window — expect every feed to be full of Leonida before London even unlocks.
