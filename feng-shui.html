Feng Shui: rearrange the house with Cal before Lucia comes home

Spoiler warning: this guide describes one Chapter 1 story mission — a quiet evening at home that ends with the call every player has been waiting for. Short answer: Feng Shui (map name: Meet Cal) starts after Island Dreams at Jason's house in Key Lento. Grab a beer from the fridge, sit on the couch with Cal Hampton, then move four pieces of furniture — the TV, coffee table, couch and chair — so the house is ready for Lucia's release from prison. After that, Cal boots up his JoySystem console and you play two games, Tenebrae Lux and Street Trash III: Dezombifier. Neither game can be won, so don't grind them — quit or let them end and the mission moves on. There is no cash reward. Everything below is Reported from launch-week walkthroughs (checked 27 November 2026); Rockstar has published no official guide of its own.

Mission facts

  • Chapter: Chapter 1 — mandatory story mission, needed for 100% completion. (Reported)
  • Mission number: the 10th main mission in Chapter 1, by most trackers. (Reported)
  • Map name: Meet Cal. (Reported)
  • Given by: Cal Hampton, by phone message. (Reported)
  • Unlock: finish Island Dreams, then follow the "Meet Cal" marker. (Reported)
  • Where: Jason's house in Key Lento, Mariana County. (Reported)
  • You play: Jason. (Reported)
  • Reward: no cash reward — the JoySystem console stays set up in Jason's house. (Reported)
  • Next mission: Coming Home. (Reported)

Where Feng Shui starts

Shortly after finishing Island Dreams, a message about meeting Cal arrives and a "Meet Cal" marker appears at Jason's house in Key Lento. Go home, climb the outside stairs and walk in through the front door. Cal is already in the living room, setting up his brand-new JoySystem console under the TV. Head to the kitchen, take a beer from the fridge, then sit on the couch beside Cal. He looks around the house and decides it is not good enough for Lucia — she is due out of prison soon, and the living room needs rearranging first. (Reported)

Step by step

  1. Finish Island Dreams, then follow the "Meet Cal" marker to Jason's house in Key Lento.
  2. Walk inside, grab a beer from the fridge and sit on the couch with Cal.
  3. Move the TV to the taped spot by the sliding glass door.
  4. Move the coffee table in front of the TV — accept its position or keep adjusting it, the outcome is the same.
  5. Move the couch so it faces the door, then move the chair under the window.
  6. Sit back down with Cal and play Tenebrae Lux — you can't finish it, so quit when Jason gets bored.
  7. Play Street Trash III — you can't win it either, so die quickly or let the timer run out.
  8. Watch the evening's cutscenes through to the morning call about Lucia's release.

Before any mission like this, a manual save is cheap insurance — GTA 6 has no mission replay (Reported).

Move the furniture

You move four pieces, in this order: the TV, the coffee table, the couch and the chair. The controls are the same for each one. Walk up to the piece the objective names and press Triangle on PS5 or Y on Xbox when the Grab prompt appears, then hold L2 + R2 (PS5) or LT + RT (Xbox) together to lift it and steer with the left stick while Cal carries the other end. Blue tape on the floor marks where each piece goes — the TV goes by the sliding glass door, the couch lines up facing the door, and the chair goes under the window. Wait for the placement prompt before letting go. (Reported)

The coffee table is the only piece with a choice. Once it is down, Cal questions its position and you can pick "Let's Move It" or "It's Fine". Pick "It's Fine" (Square on PS5 / X on Xbox) and you are done straight away. Pick "Let's Move It" and Cal keeps changing his mind, having you shift the table back and forth a few times — until it ends up in exactly the same spot. The result is the same either way, so take the quick option. (Reported)

There is a short break between the coffee table and the couch while Jason and Cal talk — just wait for the conversation to finish and the next objective to appear. (Reported)

Play Tenebrae Lux

With the room rearranged, Cal boots up the JoySystem and puts on Tenebrae Lux, a black-and-white first-person exploration game by Johannes Zuch. Pick New Game and walk toward the lighthouse and the lights — the doors you reach are locked, and the small board puzzles inside are optional. You cannot finish this game. After about three minutes Jason gets bored and stops playing on his own; if you want to move on sooner, you can quit after about two and a half minutes from the game's own pause menu (Touch Pad on PS5 / View on Xbox, then Exit Game). Either way, Jason asks for something "a little less poetic" and Cal loads the next game. (Reported)

Play Street Trash III

Next up is Street Trash III: Dezombifier, a top-down zombie shooter with a timer on screen. The on-screen goal is to collect gears and reach the high-rise before the timer runs out — but you cannot win this game either. The fastest way through is to let yourself die straight away; if you would rather play, the game ends by itself when the timer runs out. Don't waste time trying to beat it. (Reported)

What happens after

After the two games, cutscenes cover the rest of Jason and Cal's evening, and in the morning Jason gets a call from Hamlet Correctional: Lucia is being released. She asks Jason to pick up clothes from her friend Andy, who lives nearby in the Keys — that pickup is where the next mission, Coming Home, begins. The JoySystem stays set up in Jason's house, so you can go back and play the two games again later. Afterwards, Cal also texts Jason a fishing spot — his fishing challenges feed the Bronze trophy "Baited My Tackle". (Reported)

Tips

  • Don't try to beat either game. Tenebrae Lux and Street Trash III are both unwinnable in this mission — quit or die fast and move on. (Reported)
  • Say "It's Fine" to the coffee table. Moving it again only adds extra back-and-forth for the same result. (Reported)
  • Follow the blue tape. Each piece of furniture has a taped rectangle on the floor — carry it there and wait for the placement prompt. (Reported)
  • Save first. There is no mission replay, so a manual save before you start lets you redo the evening if you want to try the other coffee-table choice. (Reported)

Previous and next missions

Feng Shui unlocks after Island Dreams, in which Jason dives for treasure with Kenton's bachelor party. In the order most trackers list Chapter 1, the next mission is Coming Home — Jason picks Lucia up from Hamlet Correctional. See the full list on our GTA 6 missions page. (Reported)

FAQ

How do you start Feng Shui in GTA 6?
Finish Island Dreams, then follow the Meet Cal marker to Jason's house in Key Lento and walk inside. Grab a beer from the fridge and sit on the couch with Cal. (Reported)

Do you have to win Tenebrae Lux in Feng Shui?
No. Tenebrae Lux cannot be finished — after about three minutes Jason gets bored and stops playing. You can quit sooner from the game's pause menu. (Reported)

Do you have to win Street Trash III in Feng Shui?
No. Street Trash III cannot be won either — let yourself die straight away to save time, or play until the timer runs out. (Reported)

What happens if you keep moving the coffee table in Feng Shui?
Nothing changes. You can accept the coffee table's first position or keep moving it — Cal stays undecided and it ends up in the same spot either way. (Reported)

Is there a reward for Feng Shui in GTA 6?
No cash reward. The JoySystem console stays set up in Jason's house, and the mission ends with a call that Lucia is being released from prison. (Reported)

What mission comes after Feng Shui in GTA 6?
Coming Home is the next main mission — Jason picks Lucia up from Hamlet Correctional. (Reported)
