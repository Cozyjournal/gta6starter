GTA 6 pre-order bonus: the Vintage Vice City Pack

Order GTA 6 before 20 November 2026 and you get the Vintage Vice City Pack as a pre-order bonus. After that date the bonus is gone. Confirmed

What is the Vintage Vice City Pack?

It is a bonus pack of Vice City-themed digital content bundled with qualifying pre-orders of GTA 6. Rockstar announced it as the launch pre-order incentive for both the Standard and Ultimate editions.

Who gets it?

Anyone who orders GTA 6 before 20 November 2026 — digital or boxed. Remember that boxed copies are code-in-box: there is no playable disc, the box contains a download code, and the bonus is tied to that order.

How to make sure you qualify

  • Order before the deadline — 20 November 2026 is the cut-off, and the game launches on 19 November, so the window is short.
  • Keep your order confirmation — if the bonus does not appear in-game at launch, your receipt is what support will ask for.
  • Preload early — the download is up to 212 GB, so start it as soon as your store allows. See the preload guide.

Not ordered yet? Compare editions on the price and editions page — the bonus applies to Standard ($79.99) and Ultimate ($99.99) alike.

Sources: Rockstar Store and Rockstar Support (pre-order bonus and deadline). Checked 18 November 2026.
